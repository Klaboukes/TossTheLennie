      Player 1's turn!

        Take turns

        Everyone gets 5 tosses, one after the other. Before each turn the house is fixed and Lennie goes back downstairs. Highest total wins!

        How to play

        Grab Lennie and pull him back like a slingshot, then let go to fling him through the house, out of the window or across the street into the supermarket. When he comes to rest you score: the more twisted up he is, the more points. Smashed windows, bounces and catching him in mid-air multiply it, and landing somewhere funny (the bath, the loo, the freezer...) earns a bonus.
